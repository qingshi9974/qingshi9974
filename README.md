# Han Li

Researcher at the **WeLM Team, Weixin AI**, working on post-training for multimodal foundation models. My interests include multimodal mid-training and reinforcement learning for general-purpose multimodal models and agents.

Previously, I was a research intern with the **LongCat Team** at Meituan (2024–2026). I received my bachelor’s degree (2021) and Ph.D. (2026) from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by [Prof. Hongkai Xiong](https://min.sjtu.edu.cn/En/FacultyShow/4?Vid=14).
